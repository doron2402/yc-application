# YC Application Interview: Question Bank

Ask one at a time. ★ = must be answered by the founder, with a real answer (see the quality bar below). Never skip or assume a ★ question. The interview needs **at least 20 founder answers** before drafting; there are 30 here, so a few non-★ ones can be confirmed from pasted context instead of asked.

**Team, Problem, and Market come first and get the most depth.** They decide whether YC funds a company. Everything else on the form supports them.

Each question notes the live form field(s) it feeds (see `yc-form.md`).

---

## The quality bar (apply to every ★ answer)

A ★ answer is done only when it has at least one of: **a name, a number, a date, or a thing someone actually did.** Opinions, adjectives, and plans don't count.

- Vague → ask one sharper follow-up. Still vague → ask a second, more concrete one ("Just give me the rough number, even a guess, and tell me it's a guess").
- After two follow-ups, accept it, mark it 🟡 **weak**, and carry it into the Partner Read. Don't let the interview stall, but don't pretend it's fine.
- "I don't know" on a ★ Team/Problem/Market question is a finding, not a skip: it usually means the founder has homework before the deadline. Say so.

| Half-answer | Push back with |
|---|---|
| "Small businesses" | "Which one? Name a business that has this problem." |
| "It saves time" | "How much time, for whom, per week? How do you know?" |
| "People love it" | "What did they do? Pay, come back daily, refer someone?" |
| "Huge market" | "How many customers × what price per year?" |
| "We're passionate about this" | "What have you done in this space that most people haven't?" |
| "Strong team" | "What's the hardest thing each of you has built or shipped?" |
| "Growing fast" | "What was the number 4 weeks ago, and what is it today?" |

## 1 · Team (7), emphasized

YC funds founders more than ideas. This section is where most applicants undersell themselves.

1. ★ Who are the founders? For each: name, title (CEO/CTO/…), where they live, and whether they're full-time on this now (since when, or when they will be). → *Founder profiles, worked*
2. ★ For each founder, separately: what's the most impressive thing you've built or achieved, other than this startup? Push for the number or outcome (users, revenue, rank, award, scale, speed). → *Founder profiles, video, interview*
3. ★ Why is this team the one to win this? What do you know, or who do you have access to, that makes you unusually suited to this problem? → *exp (domain expertise), video*
4. ★ Who writes the code or does the other technical work? Was any of it done by a non-founder (agency, contractor, friend)? → *others2*
5. ★ How long have the founders known each other, and how did you meet? What have you built or shipped together? Have any of you not met in person? → *meet*
6. Tell me about a time one of you hacked a non-computer system to your advantage: bent a rule, found a loophole, got something done that people said couldn't be. → *Founder profile, interview* (YC has long asked this; it shows resourcefulness)
7. Is anyone looking for a co-founder? If solo: why, and who would you add first? → *cofounder*

**Team gate:** Is there one concrete, impressive achievement per founder? Is at least one founder technical and building the product? Full-time? If not, flag it.

## 2 · Problem (4), emphasized

The form has no "problem" box, but the problem is what makes "What is your company going to make?" and "How do you know people need it?" convincing. Get it sharp here.

8. ★ What's the problem, in one sentence, without mentioning your product? Who has it, specifically: name one real person or company. → *make, exp*
9. ★ How do they deal with it today, and what does that cost them (hours, dollars, lost revenue, risk)? How often does it happen? → *exp, get*
10. ★ How do you know people need what you're making? Give the strongest evidence: what users *did* (paid, switched, built a workaround, begged for access), plus how many people you've talked to. → *exp*
11. ★ Why now? What changed (technology, AI, regulation, behavior, cost) that makes this possible or urgent today? → *exp, get*

**Problem gate:** Is it painful, frequent, costly, with evidence from real people's behavior?

## 3 · Product & Company (4)

12. ★ What is your company going to make? Describe it to a smart friend outside your industry: who uses it, and what do they do with it on day one? → *make*
13. ★ Company name, website, product link (and test login if needed), and a demo video or screen recording (≤ 3 min) if you have one. → *name, url, productLink, productCreds, demo*
14. Say what the company does in one sentence, your way. (I'll cut it to 50 characters.) → *describe*
15. Where do the founders live now, and where would the company be based after YC? Why there? → *where, wherewhy*

## 4 · Progress (6)

16. ★ How far along are you? (1. Idea · 2. Building, no users · 3. Launched, users, no revenue · 4. Revenue) If not launched: when will people be able to use a version? → *howfar, whenbeta*
17. ★ How long has each founder been working on this, and how much of that full-time? → *worked*
18. ★ Are people using it? How many active users or customers (define "active"), how many pay, who pays you the most and how much? What was the number 4 weeks ago? → *stage, usernums*
19. ★ Revenue: how much per month right now (not cumulative, not GMV)? Last 3 months, month by month. Where does it come from: break down by product/customer/consulting. Week-over-week or month-over-month growth? → *revenue, revenueamount, revenuesource, growthrate*
20. What's your tech stack, including AI models and AI coding tools? Is there a coding-agent session (e.g. a Claude Code `/export`) you're proud of and could attach? → *techstack, codingAgentSession*
21. Have you applied to YC before? Same idea (what changed?) or different (why pivot, what did you learn?). Any other accelerator or incubator, past or committed? → *since, acc*

## 5 · Market & Competition (5), emphasized

22. ★ Who are your competitors, including the status quo (spreadsheets, agencies, doing nothing)? Name them. → *get*
23. ★ What do you understand about this business that they don't? A non-obvious truth about the customer, distribution, technology, or timing. "We're better/faster/cheaper" isn't it; keep digging. → *get*
24. ★ How do or will you make money? Who pays, what model, what price per customer per year? → *money*
25. ★ How big could this get? Count it bottom-up: how many customers like your first one exist × annual price = first market. Then: what's the bigger market you expand into, and how big is that? → *money*
26. What other ideas did you seriously consider? (YC often funds one from this list.) → *ideas*

**Market gate:** Bottom-up math, a credible path to a $1B+ market (or a clear reason the wedge expands), and a real insight about competitors.

## 6 · Equity & Money (2)

27. ★ Have you formed any legal entity, anywhere? List each (e.g. Delaware C Corp) and where. Equity split in % between each founder (with title), employees, and anyone else, or the planned split if not incorporated. → *incyet, inctype, incpercent, percent2*
28. ★ Have you raised money? For each investment: investor, amount, valuation cap or pre-money, security (SAFE, note, stock), date. Total raised. Cash in the bank, monthly spend, runway in months. Raising now? Details. → *investyet, incinvest, investamount, bank, burn, runway, currentlyraising, raisingdetails*

## 7 · Curious & Batch (2)

29. What convinced you to apply now? Did someone encourage you (name the alum)? Any YC events? How did you hear about YC? Referral code? → *whyapply, howhear, referralCode*
30. Upcoming batch, or a future one (usually only if you're a student finishing school)? Why? → *batchPreference, futureBatchName, futureBatchWhy*

## Founder video (after the interview, before drafting)

The video is **required** and YC calls it "an important part of the application". Always write the script (see `writing-guide.md`), then ask whether they want changes.

## Useful follow-ups

- "Since when? What was it a month before?"
- "Name one of them. What exactly did they do?"
- "If I called that customer, what would they say?"
- "What's the number? Even rough. Say it's an estimate."
- "Why hasn't someone already done this?"
- "What do you know that a smart person in this industry gets wrong?"
- "What's the one thing you're proudest of building, ever?"
